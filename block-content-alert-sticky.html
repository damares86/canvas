Sticky alerts are an effective way to surface important announcements, promotions, or site-wide notices that remain fully visible as users scroll through your content. The Canvas sticky alert block is lightweight, easily dismissible, and fully responsive across all screen sizes and mobile devices. You can customize the alert color scheme, call-to-action button style, text content, and overall messaging to perfectly match your brand identity. Use it to highlight a new product launch, a limited-time promotional offer, a critical maintenance update, or any key message you want every visitor to see without disrupting their overall browsing experience on your site.
