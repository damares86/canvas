Getting There

Camping & Travel

Herdade do Vento, Alentejo

150km south-east of Lisbon. GPS coordinates provided at ticket confirmation. No public address listed to preserve the site.

Festival Shuttle

Coaches depart from Lisbon Santa Apolonia at 14:00 on Friday. Return Sunday night from 01:00. Tickets: 18 EUR each way.

Drive + Park

Free secured car parking on-site. Carpooling forum accessible via your ticket portal. Limit one vehicle per 4 passes.

Camping

General camping included with all passes. Glamping pods (limited) available at checkout. Check-in opens Thursday 17:00.

Leave No Trace

Zero-waste policy. Reusable cup deposit at entrance. No single-use plastic on site. Compost toilets across all zones.
