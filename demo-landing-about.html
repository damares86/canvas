What is Canvas

Canvas is a modern wallet app built to simplify how you store, send, and manage money. Whether you are funding a side project or running a growing business, it brings your accounts, cards, and payments together in one secure, easy-to-use place that works wherever you do.

We built Canvas around the people who use it. Real-time notifications, instant transfers, and friendly support mean help is always close at hand. Every feature is shaped by listening to our community, so the app keeps getting better with each release and stays genuinely useful day to day.

Behind the scenes, Canvas runs on fast, reliable technology that keeps your data safe and your payments quick. Smart automation handles the routine work, so budgeting, reminders, and recurring transfers happen on their own while you focus on what matters most to you.

From freelancers to families, thousands of people trust Canvas to handle their everyday finances. Set goals, track spending, and watch your savings grow with clear insights. Wherever your next project takes you, Canvas is ready to move at the same pace you do, without slowing down.
