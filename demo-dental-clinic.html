Our philosophy

We rebuilt the dental visit from scratch, with calm as the foundation.

Every surface, every tool, every minute of your appointment is intentional. Slip-Medical-grade surfaces you would expect in a surgical suite, paired with the warmth of oak, linen and natural light streaming in from the park.

You will never hear a drill before you hear a hello. Our dentists sit down with you first, sketch the plan on a tablet, and only pick up an instrument once you have said yes.

Your visit, step by step

Four steps, no surprises.

01 · Discussion

A 25-minute conversation with your dentist. No chairs, no instruments, just coffee and what brought you in.

02 · Diagnostics

Low-dose 3D scan, digital intra-oral scan, and a smile-line photo set. All completed in under 20 minutes.

03 · Plan on a tablet

We sketch the treatment plan with you on a tablet. Phased, priced, and yours to walk away with.

04 · Calm treatment

Heated chairs, weighted blanket, your playlist. Your dentist checks in at every step. Forget you are here.

05 · Written summary

Before you leave, a printed and emailed visit summary: what was done, what comes next, and the exact cost of each step.

06 · 7-day follow-up

A short check-in call a week after any major treatment. No charge, no upsell. Just making sure you are healing well.

Questions, answered

The small print, before you arrive.

Anything else you want to know before booking, send us a note. We answer within one business hour.

Ask a question
Do you offer same-day emergency appointments?
Yes. CanvasVital reserves daily windows for urgent care. Call our front desk before noon and we will see you the same day.
Are your treatments suitable for anxious patients?
Every operatory uses noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blankets and optional nitrous oxide. Our dentists are trained in gentle, slow-pace technique.
Do you accept dental insurance?
We accept most major PPO plans and offer transparent self-pay bundles with no surprise line items. Bring your member card to the first visit.
How long is the first visit?
Plan for 75 minutes: 25 minutes of conversation, 20 minutes of diagnostics, and 30 minutes to walk through your tailored plan.
Where are you located?
218 Beacon Street, Boston MA. Garage parking validated, and five minutes from Back Bay station.
