How do I become an author?
Becoming a Canvas author starts with a free ThemeForest account and a quick application form. Once approved, you can upload your first item for review, choose between exclusive and non-exclusive licensing, and set your own prices. Our editorial team checks every submission for quality, then publishes approved items straight to the marketplace for buyers worldwide.
Helpful Resources for Authors
We give every author a full library of resources to help your items succeed. Start with our authoring guide and coding standards, then browse video walkthroughs that cover previews, documentation, and submission tips. An active community forum lets you ask questions and share feedback, while the help center answers everything about payouts, licensing, and keeping your published items up to date.
How much money can I make?
Your earnings depend on item quality, pricing, and demand, and there is no upper limit. Authors keep a share of every sale that grows as their lifetime earnings increase, rewarding consistent contributors. Popular items can sell hundreds of times each month, while a strong portfolio builds steady passive income. You set your own prices and watch real-time sales data from your dashboard.
