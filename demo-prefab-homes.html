The Case for Prefab

Why not build the old way?

Because the numbers tell a different story. And so does the planet.

40%

Faster to move-in

Factory production runs parallel to site preparation. Our standard 108m2 home goes from signed contract to move-in key in 14 weeks, versus 36+ months for traditional build.

0net

Net-zero ready, standard

Every Modul home ships with Passivhaus-grade insulation, thermal bridge elimination, and MVHR ventilation. Solar and battery storage are available as add-ons. Your energy bill, not our problem.

62%

Less construction waste

Precision CNC cutting in our factory means near-zero material offcuts. Scrap timber is chipped and recycled. No skip bins on your site, no neighbour complaints, no environmental guilt.

1price

Fixed cost. No surprises.

Traditional builds average 27% over budget. Every Modul home is priced at contract signing, with the full spec locked. What you sign is what you pay. Period.

The Process

From design call to move-in.

Four stages. One fixed price. Zero surprises.

1

Permits

We handle planning permission, building warrants, and utility connections. Our in-house team navigates every council requirement on your behalf.

2-4 weeks
2

Factory Build

Precision CNC production in our climate-controlled Swedish factory. Every panel, joint, and fitting crafted to millimetre tolerance while your site is being prepared.

6-8 weeks
3

Site Install

Panels arrive flat-packed. A dedicated Modul install crew assembles your home on-site in 3-5 days. Weathertight on day one. Services connected within the week.

1-2 weeks
4

Move In

Final snagging, commissioning of MVHR and heating system, and your handover walkthrough. We leave when you're fully satisfied. Then you call this place home.

1 week

Sustainability

Built to leave less.

Every Modul home meets the highest environmental standards, as standard. Not a premium add-on.

Passivhaus

Certified to the German Passivhaus Institut standard. Up to 90% reduction in heating energy demand.

BREEAM Outstanding

All standard models achieve BREEAM Outstanding, the highest rating in the UK's sustainability assessment method.

FSC Certified

Every structural timber comes from certified sustainably managed forests. Chain of custody tracked from forest to panel.

Energy A+++

EU energy performance rating of A+++ on every model. With our solar add-on, many homes achieve positive energy generation.

Water Neutral

Greywater recycling and rainwater harvesting systems are fitted as standard, reducing mains water demand by up to 55%.
