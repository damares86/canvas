Frequently Asked Questions

We have answered a wide range of Questions for your Convenience

Q. How do I become an author?

To become an author, register a free account and submit your first item for review. Our team evaluates quality, code standards, and documentation. Once approved, your item goes live in the marketplace where it can reach thousands of buyers worldwide. The process typically takes five to seven business days from submission to approval.

Q. Helpful Resources for Authors

The Author Help Center provides step-by-step guides covering everything from setting up your first portfolio to understanding the sales dashboard. You will also find video tutorials, a dedicated author forum where peers share tips, and a monthly newsletter highlighting platform updates, promotional opportunities, and new tools to help grow your passive income stream as a creative professional on the marketplace.

Q. How much money can I make?

Earnings vary based on item quality, category demand, and how actively you promote your portfolio. Top authors earn between $5,000 and $50,000 per month, while new authors typically see their first sale within the first two weeks of publishing. Our non-exclusive license lets you sell on other platforms simultaneously, so there is no ceiling on your potential income. Consistent updates and responsive support tend to drive the highest long-term sales volume.

Q. An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors

Our marketplace connects creative authors with millions of buyers seeking premium digital assets. Whether you create themes, plugins, graphics, or audio tracks, each category has an active audience. Exclusive author status unlocks higher royalty rates and featured placement opportunities.

Q. What Images, Videos, Code or Music Can I Use in my Items?

You may include assets that you own outright or that are covered by a royalty-free, commercial license. Free stock photo sites like Unsplash and Pexels are acceptable, provided the license permits resale in bundled products. Music and code libraries must carry an MIT, CC0, or similarly permissive license. Always document every third-party asset in your item description so buyers know exactly what is and is not included. Items failing to declare licensed components will be rejected during review.

Q. Can I use trademarked names in my items?

Generally, trademarked names and logos are off-limits unless you hold a written license from the brand owner. Using a recognizable logo as a placeholder in a mockup or demo screenshot is permitted only if the preview file clearly states the logo is not included in the purchase. Items that incorporate trademarked assets without disclosure will be removed from the marketplace and may result in account suspension pending a full compliance review by our dedicated author support and quality control team.

If in doubt, swap branded assets for generic equivalents before submitting. Our review team specifically checks demo content for unlicensed trademarks, and items flagged during review are returned to the author for correction before going live.

Q. Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income

Share your unique referral link across blog posts, YouTube tutorials, social media profiles, and email newsletters to attract new buyers and authors. Each time someone you refer makes a qualifying purchase within 30 days of clicking your link, you earn a percentage commission on the sale. Creating honest, educational content that walks viewers through the marketplace and highlights specific items you have built consistently converts far better than generic promotional posts or paid ads alone.
