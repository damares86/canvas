About

The Eye Behind the Lens

Alex Mercer has spent 15+ years documenting humanity's most profound moments — from mountain-top elopements in Patagonia to boardroom portraits in Manhattan. His work has appeared in Vogue, National Geographic Traveler, and The New York Times. Based in New York, available worldwide.

Every image I make is a negotiation between light and shadow, between the decisive moment and the patience to wait for it. I believe photographs should make you feel something — not just remember it.
