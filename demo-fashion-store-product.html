A wardrobe cornerstone that refuses to be seasonal. The Ivory Linen Blazer is cut from pre-washed European linen in a relaxed silhouette that moves with you. Unlined for breathability, finished with patch pockets, and designed to crease naturally because that is what linen does.

  • 100% pre-washed European linen
  • Relaxed fit, true to size
  • Two welt chest pockets, two patch hip pockets
  • Single-button closure
  • Unlined for a lighter feel
  • Ethically made in Porto, Portugal
  • Machine wash cold, gentle cycle
  • Lay flat or hang to dry
  • Iron on medium heat while slightly damp
  • Do not bleach or dry clean
  • Linen softens beautifully with every wash

Relaxed cut. Most customers size down one from their usual. Model is 5'9" and wears size M. Shoulder seam falls slightly past the natural shoulder for an effortless drape.

This blazer is certified by the Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS). Our linen is grown without pesticides in Belgium and France. The factory in Porto pays living wages and holds SA8000 certification for social responsibility.
