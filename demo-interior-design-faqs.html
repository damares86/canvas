FAQs

All your Questions answered in one place
Q.How do I become an author?
Becoming a Canvas interior design author starts with submitting a portfolio of your finished room concepts and mood boards through our studio dashboard. Our curators review your spatial planning, material palettes, and styling approach, then guide you through the onboarding process so your projects can be published, featured, and shared with clients across the platform.
Q.Can I use trademarked names in my items?
You are welcome to reference well-known furniture brands, paint manufacturers, and material suppliers when they genuinely appear in your room schemes, since accurate product details help clients source the look. However, do not present trademarked names as your own or imply official endorsement. Always credit each brand correctly within your project specifications and keep any logos or proprietary artwork out of your uploaded design imagery unless you hold the rights to use them.
Q.How much money can I make?
Earnings depend on the scope of each interior project, the number of rooms you style, and how many clients commission your concepts. Designers who consistently publish polished mood boards, detailed furniture layouts, and complete material schedules tend to attract repeat bookings and premium consultations. Many of our authors grow steady monthly income by offering tiered packages, from single-room refreshes to full residential makeovers, alongside paid revisions and sourcing services.
Q.How can I get support for an item which isn't working correctly?
If a downloadable plan, 3D render, or material list is not opening or displaying correctly, first confirm you are using the latest version from your account downloads. Clear your browser cache, try an alternate browser, and check that any required design software is up to date. Should the issue continue, contact our studio support team with your order number and a short description, and we will reissue the files or resolve the rendering problem promptly.
Q.An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors
This guide introduces new interior designers to how our marketplace links your room concepts with homeowners and developers seeking fresh styling. You will learn how listings are grouped by room type and aesthetic, and how to position your portfolio so the right clients find your work.
Q.Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income
Grow your referral earnings by sharing favourite room transformations and styling tips with your personal invite link. Encourage happy clients to recommend your studio profile and feature before-and-after photos so each referral expands your design community.
