THE DEVELOPER

Built on Three Decades of Excellence

Harrington Group Est. 1994 — Melbourne

Founded in Melbourne in 1994 by James Harrington, the Harrington Group has spent three decades defining what premium residential development looks like in Australia. From its first boutique terrace conversion in South Yarra to landmark tower projects reshaping the CBD skyline, the Group has never wavered from a singular conviction: that the quality of your home shapes the quality of your life.

With a portfolio spanning $4.2 billion across Melbourne's most sought-after neighbourhoods — South Yarra, Fitzroy, Docklands, and the CBD — Harrington Group has delivered over 2,400 residences to discerning buyers. Each project is held to the same exacting standard: architecturally resolved, thoughtfully amenitised, and built to endure.

Harrington Residences represents the Group's most ambitious undertaking to date — 142 homes across 35 levels, designed in collaboration with Fender Katsalidis Architects and interiors by Studio Griffiths. A culmination of thirty years of craft, delivered to the next generation of Melbourne residents.
