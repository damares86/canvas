How do I become an author?
To join as an author, submit a work sample via the author application page. Our team reviews each submission for quality, originality, and commercial appeal. Approved applicants receive an invitation to create their profile and start uploading items. The review process typically takes 5 to 10 business days, and you can track your application status in your account dashboard.
Helpful Resources for Authors
Our Author Resource Center includes guides, video tutorials, and best-practice articles on pricing, portfolio optimization, and search visibility. You can join private author forums to connect with peers and share tips. Monthly webinars hosted by top-selling authors provide direct access to strategies for growing your marketplace earnings and building a sustainable long-term income from your digital portfolio.
How much money can I make?
Earnings vary based on item quality, demand, and how actively you promote your portfolio. Top authors earn thousands of dollars monthly. New authors typically see income grow steadily as their catalog expands and positive reviews accumulate. We pay competitive royalty rates and provide real-time earnings reports so you can monitor performance and adjust your pricing strategy accordingly.
Can I offer my items for free on a promotional basis?
Yes, you can make items available free during limited promotional windows to build visibility and gather reviews. Promotional offers are arranged through your author dashboard and follow our standard licensing terms. Free downloads still count toward your item statistics, improving its ranking and discoverability. This is an effective way for new authors to attract buyers and establish a positive reputation.
An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors
Our marketplace connects authors with global buyers seeking premium digital assets. You set your own prices and retain full creative ownership of your work. Items are reviewed for quality before going live. You benefit from built-in SEO, a trusted payment infrastructure, and marketing campaigns that bring millions of qualified buyers to discover and purchase items from your portfolio every month.
How does the Tuts+ Premium affiliate program work?
Our affiliate program rewards you with a commission for every new subscriber you refer. Sign up, copy your unique referral link, and share it on your blog, social channels, or newsletter. You earn a percentage of each referred subscription for its lifetime. Real-time tracking reports show clicks, conversions, and total earnings so you can optimize your promotional content and maximize referral revenue.
What Images, Videos, Code or Music Can I Use in my Items?
Only include third-party assets carrying licenses that permit commercial use. Reputable stock libraries with Extended or Commercial licenses are acceptable. Open-source code is allowed when its license is compatible with our terms. Music and video clips must also be properly licensed. Document your asset sources in the upload notes and include attribution files wherever the license requires it.
Can I use trademarked names in my items?
Using trademarked names, logos, or brand identifiers without written permission from the holder is not permitted. This applies to mockups, theme previews, and demo content. Replace real brand assets with generic alternatives before submitting. Violations discovered after approval result in item removal and may affect your author standing. Review our legal guidelines or contact author support when uncertain.
Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income
Maximize referral income by targeting channels where your audience actively seeks what you promote. Tutorials, comparison articles, and demo videos consistently outperform generic banners. Authors who publish new referral-focused content twice a month see significantly better conversion rates. Track your top-performing links and double down on the formats and platforms that drive the most new sign-ups.
How can I get support for an item which isn't working correctly?
Visit the item's support page and submit a detailed ticket including your browser version, error messages, and steps to reproduce the issue. Most authors respond within 24 to 48 business hours. The public comment section lets you post questions and help other buyers facing the same problem. For purchase-critical issues, our marketplace support team is available via live chat Monday through Friday.
How do I pay for items on the Marketplaces?
We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and several regional payment methods. Add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, and select your preferred option. Transactions are processed securely and payment details are never stored on our servers. Pre-loading your account with credits enables faster checkout. Once confirmed, your files are instantly available from the downloads section of your account.
