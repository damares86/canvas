Steps of Works

See How I Do.

From the first planning call to the final hand-edited gallery, here is how a shoot with me actually unfolds, step by step, so you always know what comes next.

Updated Lenses:

I shoot on the latest full-frame bodies and fast prime lenses, keeping every kit cleaned, calibrated and ready for any light. Glass is updated each season.


Backup bodies and cards travel to every single shoot.

Film Making:

Cinematic films that capture the whole day in honest motion.


Alongside stills I produce short films of weddings, travels and brand stories, edited with a colour-graded, cinematic feel that brings each moment back to life.

&

Most Edited Tools:

Every frame is hand-edited by me, never a batch filter.


I cull, retouch and grade in Lightroom and Photoshop, finishing each image by hand so skin tones, colour and mood stay true to how the moment actually felt.
