Some of your Questions:

Browse the answers below to learn how our car marketplace works, from listing your vehicle and pricing it right to payments, dealer support, and earning through referrals.

Marketplace (4)

How do I become an author?
Becoming a listed seller is simple. Create a free account, verify your identity and dealership details, then submit your first car listing with photos and specifications. Our team reviews every submission, and once approved your vehicles go live for buyers to browse. Most accounts are activated within one business day, so you can start selling right away.
Helpful Resources for Authors
We provide a full library of resources to help you sell with confidence. Inside your seller dashboard you will find listing guides, photography tips, pricing benchmarks drawn from recent local sales, and templates for writing compelling vehicle descriptions. Our knowledge base also covers payment handling, buyer communication, and best practices for arranging viewings and test drives safely. If you ever need more, our support team is available to walk you through any part of the selling process from start to finish.
How much money can I make?
Your earnings depend on the vehicles you list, their condition, and how competitively you price them. Sellers who provide thorough descriptions, quality photographs, and fair market pricing tend to sell faster and at stronger values. There is no cap on how many cars you can list, so active dealers managing larger inventories naturally earn more over time. We also publish current local sales data in your dashboard, helping you set realistic prices that attract buyers while protecting your margin on every transaction you complete here.
How do I pay for items on the Marketplaces?
Payments are handled securely through the marketplace using trusted, encrypted gateways. Buyers can pay deposits or full amounts by card, bank transfer, or approved financing partners, depending on the seller's accepted methods shown on each listing. Funds are protected until the agreed conditions are met, giving both parties confidence throughout the purchase. For larger transactions, our team can guide you through documentation and secure handover so the entire process stays transparent, traceable, and safe from the first enquiry to final delivery.

Authors (5)

Can I offer my items for free on a promotional basis?
Yes, you can run promotional offers on your listings whenever you like. Many sellers use limited-time price drops, free first services, or accessory bundles to draw attention to a model. Just edit the listing, set the terms and end date, and the discount shows clearly to buyers. Promotions help you move stock quickly and build a loyal following.
How do I become an author?
Getting started as a seller takes only a few minutes. Register your account, confirm your dealership information, and upload your first vehicle with clear photos and an honest description. Each listing is checked by our team before it appears publicly, keeping buyer trust high. Approvals are usually done within a day, letting you connect with serious buyers.
An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors
Our marketplace connects independent sellers and established dealers with buyers searching for their next vehicle. As a seller you gain access to a large, motivated audience, simple listing tools, and secure messaging to handle enquiries. Listings can include detailed specifications, full photo galleries, and pricing history so buyers make informed decisions. Whether you are selling a single car or managing a large inventory, the platform scales to suit you and helps your vehicles reach the right people quickly and reliably.
How do I pay for items on the Marketplaces?
Completing a purchase is straightforward and secure. Once you agree on a price with the seller, payment is processed through the platform's protected gateway using your preferred method, whether that is a card, bank transfer, or an approved finance plan. Your funds are held safely until the vehicle and paperwork are confirmed, which shields both buyer and seller from fraud. For high-value deals our support team can assist with verification and a smooth, well-documented handover, so you can buy your next car with total peace of mind.
Helpful Resources for Authors
There are plenty of resources to support you at every step of selling. Your dashboard includes step-by-step listing walkthroughs, guidance on staging and photographing a car, and current market data to help you price competitively. You will also find advice on negotiating fairly, completing paperwork, and arranging safe handovers with buyers. Should a question fall outside the guides, our dedicated seller support team responds promptly and can advise on anything from documentation to finalizing a successful sale.

Item Discussion (3)

What Images, Videos, Code Can I Use in my Items?
You may use any photos and videos you own or have permission to publish for your listings. We recommend bright, high-resolution images showing the exterior, interior, engine bay, and any notable wear. Walkthrough videos are encouraged and boost buyer confidence. Avoid stock photos that misrepresent the car, as accurate media keeps listings compliant.
Can I use trademarked names in my items?
You may reference the make, model, and trim of any vehicle you are genuinely listing, as buyers need this information to search and compare cars accurately. What you must not do is imply an official partnership or endorsement from a manufacturer that does not exist, or use brand logos in a misleading way. Keep descriptions factual and tied to the actual car, and you will stay well within our listing rules and applicable trademark guidelines while still being fully discoverable in search.
How can I get support for an item?
Support is available whenever you need it, both before and after a sale. From any listing or your account dashboard you can message the seller directly with questions about a vehicle's history, condition, or specifications. If you require help from us, our customer care team can be reached by phone, email, or live chat and typically responds within a few hours. We also maintain a detailed help center covering payments, viewings, finance, and disputes, so most questions are resolved quickly and you are never left without guidance.

Affiliates (2)

How does the Tuts+ Premium affiliate program work?
Our affiliate program rewards you for referring buyers and sellers to the marketplace. Share your unique link on social media, your website, or by email, and earn a commission on every qualifying transaction. Earnings are tracked in real time from your dashboard and paid monthly once you reach the threshold. It is a simple way to monetize your audience.
Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income
To grow your referral earnings, share your link where car buyers and sellers naturally gather, such as enthusiast forums, local community groups, and your own social channels. Pair the link with helpful content like buying guides or honest reviews so it feels genuine rather than promotional. Consistency matters, so post regularly and track which sources convert best from your dashboard. Focusing your effort on the channels that perform will steadily increase the income you earn from every successful referral.
