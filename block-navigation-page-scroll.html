Section 1

Welcome to the page-scroll navigation demo, where smooth anchored jumps turn a long page into an effortless, continuous journey. The dotted menu fixed to the side of the viewport tracks your position as you scroll, highlighting the active section in real time so visitors always know exactly where they are in the story. Each menu dot expands on hover to reveal its descriptive label, keeping the overall layout clean and uncluttered while staying instantly readable when you need it. Click any item and the page glides to the matching section using a configurable easing curve, with an adjustable offset that accounts for any sticky header sitting at the top. This pattern is ideal for one-page portfolios, product launches, marketing landing pages, and storytelling layouts that benefit from a single, uninterrupted narrative. Because every section shares one URL with hash fragments, links remain shareable and bookmarkable, and the browser back button behaves exactly as users expect it to. Built entirely with native Canvas components, the scroll menu requires no extra plugins and inherits your chosen theme color automatically. You can position it on the left or right, switch freely between dotted and labelled styles, and fine-tune the animation speed to match your brand. The result is a navigation experience that feels modern, intuitive, and genuinely polished across desktop and mobile screens alike.

Section 2

Scroll-driven page menus solve a common problem for long pages: keeping orientation simple without forcing visitors to hunt for a traditional top navigation bar. As the reader moves steadily down the page, the active section indicator updates automatically, giving constant visual feedback about progress through the content. The menu sits unobtrusively against the very edge of the viewport, expanding only when it is hovered so it never competes with the rest of your design. Smooth scrolling replaces abrupt jumps, and the easing option lets you choose anything from a gentle, gradual slide to a snappy, energetic transition. An offset value keeps target headings clear of any fixed header, so anchored sections always land in a comfortable, readable position on screen. This approach pairs beautifully with full-height hero blocks, feature grids, pricing tables, testimonials, and call-to-action panels arranged in a logical sequence. Everything is responsive by default, collapsing gracefully on smaller devices where a compact trigger icon quietly takes over. Because it is part of the Canvas framework, the component integrates seamlessly with everything else, sharing typography, spacing, and color tokens for a consistent look throughout. Setting it up takes only a data attribute or two, leaving you free to focus on writing great content rather than wiring up scripts.
