Treatments

A full menu of dental care, honestly priced.

From a six-month hygiene appointment to a full-arch restoration, every CanvasVital treatment is phased, transparent, and yours to approve before we begin.

01 · Cosmetic

Smile Design

A multi-visit journey: 3D face scan, digital veneer preview, then a no-prep or minimal-prep protocol. We model your new smile against your facial proportions before any instrument touches your enamel.

  • Digital veneer preview
  • Minimal-prep porcelain
  • Composite bonding add-on
  • Bite adjustment included
From $2,800 per tooth
02 · Cosmetic

In-chair Whitening

A single 75-minute session with low-sensitivity peroxide gel and LED activation. Leave with teeth up to six shades brighter. Includes a take-home maintenance kit.

  • 75-minute appointment
  • Low-sensitivity formula
  • Custom take-home tray
  • 12-month shade check
$450 per session
03 · Orthodontic

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Planned using iterative 3D simulation, with attachments placed the same day. Our Diamond Provider status means we have completed over 1,400 cases and can resolve complex malocclusion without braces.

  • iTero 3D scan, no impressions
  • Diamond Provider experience
  • Average 7-month treatment
  • Vivera retainer included
From $5,200 full treatment
04 · Surgical

Dental Implants

Single-tooth to full-arch implant restoration. Computer-guided placement for sub-millimetre precision, with same-day temporary crowns where bone density allows. We work with biocompatible titanium and zirconia fixtures.

  • 3D-guided placement
  • Same-day temporaries
  • Titanium or zirconia
  • 10-year workmanship warranty
From $3,600 per implant
05 · Preventive

Hygiene & Prevention

Six-month recall with a dedicated hygienist. Includes salivary pH testing, an oral-cancer screen, an airflow polish, and a personalised home-care routine. Designed to catch problems before they exist.

  • 45-minute appointment
  • Salivary diagnostics
  • Oral-cancer screen
  • Home-care routine
$195 per visit
06 · Comfort

Sedation Dentistry

Nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation, designed for anxious or long-time-in-the-chair patients. You stay responsive but profoundly relaxed. Available as an add-on to any treatment.

  • Nitrous oxide available
  • Oral conscious sedation
  • Recovery lounge afterwards
  • Escort-home policy
From $185 per session
