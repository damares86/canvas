The Only Rules

FOUR SIMPLE
COMMITMENTS

The challenge works because of the rules. Simple, non-negotiable, and the exact reason 89% of our challengers finish what they start.

01
Show Up Every Day

Complete your daily workout video, no matter what. Missed a day? Make it up the same week. Consistency over perfection - but perfection is the goal.

02
Follow the Meal Guide

Stick to the daily nutrition plan 90% of the time. Life happens. But the guide is designed to be flexible and realistic, not restrictive and miserable.

03
Check In With the Group

Post your daily workout confirmation in the group. Even on the hard days. Especially on the hard days. Your check-in might be someone else's motivation to keep going.

04
Take Your Progress Photos

Day 1 and Day 30 photos are non-negotiable. Keep them private or share them with the group. Either way, you need to see your own transformation to believe it.
