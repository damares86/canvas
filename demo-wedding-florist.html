Our Commitment

Seasonal Flowers and Our Sustainability Pledge

We believe beautiful weddings should not cost the earth. Every choice we make — from the farms we source from to the floral foam we refuse to use — reflects our commitment to a more sustainable floral industry.

  • We source from certified sustainable British and local farms first, supplementing only where needed.
  • We are 100% floral foam free, using chicken wire, moss, and water-retaining mechanics instead.
  • Leftover blooms are donated to hospitals, hospices, and care homes after each wedding.
  • All our packaging is recyclable or compostable. We take back all hire items and structures.
