How It Works

From Brief to
Final Delivery

Our structured production process keeps your project on time, on budget, and on brief. Most standard videos ship in 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff.

01
Week 1
Brief and Script

Discovery call, creative brief alignment, scriptwriting, and voiceover casting. We nail the message before we touch the visuals.

02
Weeks 2 to 3
Design and Storyboard

Visual style development, character design, scene storyboarding, and style frame approval. You see it before we animate it.

03
Weeks 3 to 5
Animation

Full animation production with two rounds of revisions built in. Character rigging, scene animation, and visual effects all polished to spec.

04
Weeks 5 to 6
Sound and Delivery

Professional sound design, music scoring, final color grade, and delivery in every format you need. H.264, ProRes, and social cuts included.
