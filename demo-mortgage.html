What Affects Your Rate?

Understanding these five factors helps you take control of your mortgage cost before you apply.

Credit Score
Higher scores unlock lower rates — even a 20-point difference can save thousands over the life of your loan.
Loan-to-Value Ratio
A larger down payment lowers your LTV, reducing lender risk and typically qualifying you for better pricing.
Loan Type
Conventional, FHA, VA, and jumbo loans each carry different rate structures and eligibility requirements.
Property Type
Primary residences, investment properties, and condos are priced differently by lenders based on occupancy risk.
Loan Term
Shorter terms (15-year) come with lower rates but higher monthly payments — we help you find the right balance.
