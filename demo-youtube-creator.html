About the channel

A travel channel for people who like their destinations a little strange.

I'm Mira. I left my newsroom job in 2017 to film the kind of travel stories I wanted to watch and couldn't find. Every CanvasRoam film is shot by a crew of two, edited over a month, and built around one question: what is it actually like to be there?

Three content pillars

01
Slow Stays
One place, seven days, no itinerary. The kind of travel that turns into a story.
02
Vanishing Trades
Portraits of the crafts and trades that won't exist in twenty years. Filmed with patience.
03
Off Route
Documentary episodes from places you can't find in a guidebook, with locals as co-directors.
