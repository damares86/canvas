Can I use trademarked names in my items?
You generally cannot use trademarked names, logos, or brands in your items unless you hold the proper rights or permission. Using protected marks can lead to takedown requests and account issues. When in doubt, choose original naming and generic placeholder content so your item stays fully compliant and safe for every buyer to use commercially.

You may include images, video, code, or music in your items only when you have the proper license or own the rights outright.
