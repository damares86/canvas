Questions

Asked before, answered honestly

Is this course right for total beginners?
Yes, if you have at least one project to work with, even a student or self-initiated one. The course assumes you know basic design tools but assumes nothing about portfolio strategy. If you have never designed anything yet, start with a fundamentals course first and come back.
How much time do I need each week?
Plan on 2 to 3 hours weekly for eight weeks. Each module is 36 to 63 minutes of video plus a project assignment. Students with a deadline (an interview loop, a layoff, a visa window) often compress it into four weeks.
How does the personal critique work?
In module 7 you submit your strongest case study through the course portal. Maya records a 15 to 20 minute video review within five business days, then reviews your rewrite in a second round. It is asynchronous, so time zones do not matter.
What if my best work is under NDA?
Module 2 covers exactly this. You will learn three legitimate ways to show NDA work: redaction, recreation with dummy data, and process-only case studies. About a third of students are in this situation.
Does the payment plan change what I get?
No. The 3 x $77 plan unlocks the entire course on day one, including critique rounds. The only difference is the total price ($231 versus $197) and when you pay it.
How does the 30-day guarantee actually work?
Email support within 30 days of purchase from the address you enrolled with. We refund to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. No questions, no forms, and your course access simply ends.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes, a verifiable certificate of completion once you finish all eight modules. That said, the hiring managers in module 1 will tell you the portfolio itself is the credential that matters.
