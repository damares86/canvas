Our Facilities

Everything You Need to Play Your Best

Greenfield's infrastructure is maintained to Tennis Australia tournament standards — 12 courts across two premium surfaces, professional-grade amenities, and everything a competitive or social player needs, all within one distinguished facility.

8 Hard Courts

Plexicushion surface with full-perimeter floodlighting — playable morning through to late evening, year-round.

4 Clay Courts

Imported red clay, maintained to competition grade — ideal for developing a patient, tactical baseline game.

Automated Hitting Wall

Sensor-equipped and fully bookable — a purpose-built space for solo drilling, stroke refinement, and serve practice.

Fully Equipped Gym

Open 6am–10pm daily with strength, conditioning, and stretch zones — designed around the demands of on-court performance.

Members Clubhouse

Bar, lounge, and a sheltered dining terrace overlooking the courts — the social heart of Greenfield since 1962.

Pro Shop

Expert racquet stringing, premium apparel, and a curated range of accessories — everything you need, stocked courtside.

Junior Academy

Grow the Game.
Build Champions.

For over 30 years, Greenfield Tennis Club has been one of Melbourne's most trusted junior development pathways. Our junior academy blends internationally recognised Tennis Australia methodology with the warmth of a genuine club community — turning curious beginners into confident, competitive players who love the game for life.

Mini Red · Ages 5–8

Foam balls, scaled-down courts, and pure fun — introducing children to movement, coordination, and a lifelong love of tennis in a low-pressure, playful environment.

Junior Orange & Green · Ages 9–12

Technique fundamentals, stroke development, court positioning, and entry-level mini-tournaments — where competitive instinct first takes shape.

Junior Squad · Ages 13–18

High-performance training sessions, inter-club competition, and a structured senior pathway — for juniors ready to compete at state and national levels.

Club Life

More Than Tennis — A Community

From post-match drinks to gala dinners, Greenfield's social calendar is as lively as its courts.

Every Friday · 5 – 8 pm
Sunset Social Mixer

Wind down the week with complimentary drinks, a relaxed round-robin format, and the best sunset views on the club terrace. No booking required — just show up and play.

October · Black Tie
Annual Club Gala Dinner

Greenfield's most anticipated night of the year — black-tie attire, live music, a three-course dinner, and the presentation of the Member of the Year and Championship awards.

November · All Welcome
Charity Open Day

Community courts thrown open to all — free hit-ups, a sausage sizzle, kids' activities, and a silent auction raising funds for local youth tennis development programs.
