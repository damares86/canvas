Play Video with Modal Lightbox Button

This card layout pairs a clean author header with a generous excerpt, then surfaces a prominent call to action that opens the featured video inside a modal lightbox. It is ideal for tutorials, interviews, and product walkthroughs where an inviting thumbnail does most of the persuasion. The flexible structure keeps the title, summary, and play button balanced across every screen size, while the muted body color and roomy line spacing make longer introductions genuinely comfortable to read. Drop in your own copy here to set context, tease the highlights, and give visitors a clear reason to press play before they ever leave the page.
