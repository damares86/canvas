A word from the founder

I trained at Harvard. I learned the rest at a kitchen table.

My grandmother was terrified of dentists. She would not go until the pain became unbearable, and then quietly. I founded CanvasVital with one belief: a clinic should feel like a kitchen, not a surgery. Sit, talk, plan, then decide together.

Today our team is 14 people across six operatories. We do everything from porcelain veneers to full-arch implant restoration, but the rule stays the same: never an instrument before a hello.
