01

Does the AI really tailor my resume to a specific job?

Yes. Paste any job description and CanvasResume rewrites your summary, reorders your skills, and rewrites bullet points to mirror the role's priorities and keywords. The full rewrite runs in under 30 seconds, and you can accept, edit, or revert any line before exporting.

AI Tailoring
02

Will my resume actually pass ATS systems?

Every template is built on clean, single-column HTML that Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and Taleo parse without breaking. The live ATS score checks keyword coverage, section structure, and formatting in real time, so you can see exactly why a bot might reject you before you apply.

ATS Optimisation
03

Can I export to PDF, Word, and Google Docs?

Yes, one click each. The PDF is ATS-friendly with selectable text. The .docx stays editable for mentors. The Google Docs link syncs instantly and keeps your formatting. Same content, three destinations, no copy-paste fixes.

Export Formats
04

Do you actually deliver 3× more interviews?

Across 2.4M users in the last 24 months, Pro plan users get 3.1× the interview rate of their previous, non-tailored resume over a 30-day window. Individual results vary by industry, role, and seniority, but the average is real, not marketing fluff.

05

Is the free plan actually useful?

Yes. You can build one polished, ATS-clean resume on a free template and export it to PDF. The upgrade matters once you're applying to multiple roles at once, want cover letter AI, or need LinkedIn optimization.

06

Will the AI sound like a robot wrote my resume?

No. The AI is trained on real resumes, not on LinkedIn-speak. It mirrors your voice, the way you describe your wins, and your style. Every bullet is editable in plain English, and you can switch between three tone presets: concise, conversational, or executive.

Cancel in two clicks from your account page. We never sell your data. You can export every resume as a PDF, Word file, or JSON archive at any time, even after cancellation. Deleting your account permanently removes your data within 30 days.
