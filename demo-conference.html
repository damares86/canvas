About The Conference

About Meetup

The Canvas Development Conference brings together developers, designers, and product leaders for three days of hands-on sessions, deep technical talks, and honest conversations about building modern web experiences that perform and scale.

Across keynotes, workshops, and live demos you will learn practical techniques from industry experts, sharpen your skills with the latest tools, and connect with a community of makers shaping the future of the web together.
