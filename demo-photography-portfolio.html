About

Maeve
Holloway

I photograph the world as it hesitates, the moment before everything shifts.

Based between New York and the high desert, I work across editorial, commercial, and fine art photography. My practice is built on patience, an obsession with available light, and a deep belief that restraint is its own form of expression.

After a decade shooting for publications including Aperture, New York Times Magazine, and Vogue Italia, I turned my focus toward long-form personal projects that explore solitude, memory, and the weight of quiet spaces.
