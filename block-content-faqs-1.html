How do I become an author?
To become an author, sign up for a free account and submit your first item for review. Our team evaluates each submission for quality, originality, and marketplace fit. Once approved, your item goes live and you can start earning royalties immediately. Most authors complete the process within a few business days from registration to first sale.
Helpful Resources for Authors
We provide a comprehensive author handbook covering everything from item submission to pricing strategy and buyer communication. You'll also find video tutorials, a dedicated author forum, and a monthly newsletter with platform updates. Our support team is available to answer questions about your account, earnings, and the review process at any stage.
How much money can I make?
Earnings vary based on the quality and demand of your items, your pricing, and how actively you promote your portfolio. Top authors generate consistent five-figure monthly incomes, while newcomers typically build steadily over the first few months. Your royalty rate increases as your total sales volume grows, rewarding long-term contributors with higher revenue shares.
Can I offer my items for free on a promotional basis?
Authors may periodically offer items at no charge during approved promotional periods. Free promotions must be submitted in advance through your author dashboard and are subject to marketplace guidelines. Items offered for free must still meet all quality standards, and any bundled third-party assets must carry licenses that explicitly permit free redistribution.
An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors
Our marketplaces connect creative professionals with millions of buyers worldwide. As an author, you gain immediate access to a global audience without managing your own storefront or payment processing. Each marketplace focuses on a specific content category, giving your items maximum visibility among buyers actively searching for exactly what you create and sell.
How does the Tuts+ Premium affiliate program work?
The affiliate program lets you earn a commission on every new Tuts+ Premium subscription referred through your unique tracking link. Sign up through your account dashboard to receive your link, then share it on your website, social media, or email list. Commissions are credited to your account monthly and can be withdrawn alongside your regular author earnings.
What Images, Videos, Code or Music Can I Use in my Items?
All assets included in your items must be properly licensed for commercial use and redistribution. You may use content you created yourself, assets from approved royalty-free sources, or material released under Creative Commons licenses that permit commercial use. Always document the source and license of every third-party asset in your item's description to avoid disputes.
Can I use trademarked names in my items?
Using trademarked names, logos, or brand identifiers in your items is generally not permitted without written permission from the trademark holder. This applies to item names, preview images, descriptions, and any bundled content. If your item is inspired by a brand, use generic terminology in its title and visuals to avoid potential intellectual property claims.
Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income
Maximize referral income by sharing your affiliate link in high-traffic locations such as blog posts, YouTube descriptions, and social media profiles. Create honest reviews and tutorials showcasing items you genuinely recommend, as authentic content converts far better than generic promotions. Tracking your link's performance through the dashboard helps you identify which channels drive the most sign-ups.
How can I get support for an item which isn't working correctly?
If an item is not working as described, first check the item's comments section for known issues and community solutions. If the problem persists, contact the author directly through the item page using the support tab — authors are required to respond within a reasonable timeframe. For unresolved issues, you can escalate to our marketplace team who will mediate on your behalf.
How do I pay for items on the Marketplaces?
Items can be purchased using credits loaded onto your account or directly with a major credit or debit card at checkout. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Credits never expire and can be topped up in any amount. For teams and agencies, invoice-based billing options are available through the account settings under the billing section.
