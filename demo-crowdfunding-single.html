The Canvas iDrone 2020 packs a stabilized 4K camera, intelligent obstacle avoidance and a 35-minute flight time into a frame that folds to the size of a paperback. Built for creators who travel light, it captures cinematic footage straight out of the bag.

Every iDrone ships with optical-flow positioning and a dedicated GPS module. Read the full flight-mode breakdown. Backers receive early access to firmware updates and a set of spare propellers. Our goal is to put pro-grade aerial filmmaking in the hands of independent storytellers everywhere, without the steep price tag. Pledge today and join the launch crew.

We started this campaign because the drones we loved were either toys or out of reach. The iDrone closes that gap with a serious sensor, smart flight modes and a price built for everyday creators. Your support funds the first production run and the tooling that makes it possible. Thank you for believing in the idea.

The carbon-fiber arms keep the airframe under 250 grams while staying rigid in gusty conditions. A quick-release battery means you spend more time flying and less time waiting on the ground.

Three-axis gimbal stabilization keeps every shot smooth, even during fast tracking moves. Pair the iDrone with our free companion app for one-tap cinematic presets. Explore the sample footage gallery shot by our beta pilots across four continents. This is aerial video made effortless.

Charging is handled by a single USB-C port, and the included hub tops up three batteries at once. Whether you are scouting a wedding venue or chasing a sunset over the coast, the iDrone keeps pace with your creativity from dawn to dusk.

We have spent two years refining the flight controller for buttery-smooth handling in the air. Backer feedback shaped the redesigned remote, the longer-lasting cells and the rugged travel case that ships with every unit. This is the drone our community asked us to build, and we cannot wait to put it in your hands.

Becoming a campaign creator is simple. Sign up for a free account, complete a short identity verification and submit your project for review. You can also book a free call with a campaign mentor who has run a successfully funded project of their own from start to finish.
How much money can I make?
There is no cap on what a campaign can raise. Funding depends on your goal, the strength of your idea and how actively you share it with your audience. We charge a small platform fee only on successfully funded projects, so you keep the vast majority of every pledge your backers contribute toward bringing the product to life.
Can I offer my items for free on a promotional basis?
Yes. Many creators add a limited number of free or heavily discounted early-bird rewards to build momentum in the first 48 hours. You can also offer promotional add-ons, referral perks and bundle deals. Just set the quantity and end date in your reward settings and the platform handles the rest automatically for you.
