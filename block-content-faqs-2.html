How do I become an author?
To become an author, simply sign up for a free account and submit your work through the Author Dashboard. Our review team evaluates submissions for quality, originality, and marketplace fit. Once approved, your items go live and you can start earning royalties immediately. Most applications receive a decision within five business days.
Helpful Resources for Authors
Authors have access to a dedicated resource hub featuring step-by-step upload guides, pricing recommendations, portfolio best practices, and community forums. You can also join live webinars hosted by experienced authors and browse the knowledge base for answers to common questions about publishing, licensing, and growing your sales.
How much money can I make?
Earnings vary based on your item's price, exclusivity, and sales volume. Exclusive authors earn higher royalty rates, while non-exclusive authors retain flexibility to sell elsewhere. Many top authors generate consistent monthly income by building a diverse portfolio. Your author dashboard tracks all sales, payouts, and performance metrics in real time.
Can I offer my items for free on a promotional basis?
Yes, authors may participate in approved promotional campaigns and limited-time free download events organized by the marketplace. Outside of official promotions, items must maintain their listed price. If you want to run your own promotion, contact the author support team to discuss eligible programs and ensure your campaign complies with marketplace policies.
An Introduction to the Marketplaces for Authors
The marketplace connects creative professionals with millions of buyers worldwide. Authors can list digital assets including themes, plugins, graphics, audio, and video. Each marketplace has its own review standards and category guidelines. Registering as an author is free, and you retain full control over your portfolio, pricing, and item updates after your items are approved.
How does the Tuts+ Premium affiliate program work?
As an affiliate, you earn a commission for every new Tuts+ Premium subscriber you refer. Simply grab your unique referral link from the affiliate dashboard and share it on your site, social channels, or email list. Commissions are tracked in real time, and payouts are processed monthly once you reach the minimum threshold. No sales experience is required.
What Images, Videos, Code or Music Can I Use in my Items?
You may only include assets you own outright, have licensed for commercial redistribution, or that are in the public domain. Stock imagery, third-party plugins, and music tracks must carry a license explicitly permitting resale or bundling. Always document your asset sources and include a credits file with your submission to simplify the review process.
Can I use trademarked names in my items?
Using trademarked names, logos, or brands in your items without explicit written permission from the trademark holder is not allowed. This applies to item titles, descriptions, preview images, and bundled assets. If your item is inspired by a trademarked product but does not infringe, consult the legal guidelines in the author help center before submitting.
Tips for Increasing Your Referral Income
Boost referral income by targeting audiences who are actively searching for the products you recommend. Write in-depth reviews and tutorials, embed banners in high-traffic blog posts, and share your referral links in relevant online communities. Consistent promotion, combined with seasonal offers and honest product comparisons, tends to drive higher conversion rates over time.
How can I get support for an item which isn't working correctly?
Start by visiting the item's dedicated support tab, where the author may have posted known fixes or setup guides. If the issue persists, post a detailed bug report including your browser, OS, WordPress version, and any error messages. Most authors respond within 48 hours. For items covered by official support, you can also submit a ticket directly.
How do I pay for items on the Marketplaces?
Purchases are made using credits added to your account. You can top up your credit balance using major credit cards, PayPal, or other locally supported payment methods. Once credits are in your account, checkout is instant. A payment receipt is emailed after every transaction, and your full purchase history is available in your account settings.
