The Path to Mastery

Belt Grading System

Our structured belt progressions give you clear milestones to work toward. Gradings are held every 3-4 months and are a celebration of your hard work.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Belts
  • White Belt
    Foundations of position, escapes, and basic submissions. Typical progression: 12-18 months with consistent training.
  • Blue Belt
    Solid understanding of all fundamental positions. Typical time: 1-2 years. 4 stripe system tracks progress.
  • Purple Belt
    Advanced techniques and deep conceptual understanding. Minimum 18 months at blue. Often assists with instruction.
  • Brown Belt
    High-level refinement and preparation for black belt. Minimum 1 year at purple. Competition recommended.
  • Black Belt
    Mastery of the art. Average 10 years from white. Recognized globally by IBJJF. The beginning of true understanding.
Kids Karate Belts
  • White Belt
    Basic stances, blocking, and listening skills. Focus on following instruction and dojo etiquette.
  • Yellow Belt
    Basic kata, punch and kick combinations. Introduced to sparring pads and partner drills.
  • Orange Belt
    More complex kata, self-defence scenarios, developing power and precision in techniques.
  • Green Belt
    Intermediate kata, controlled light sparring, leadership within the junior class structure.
  • Blue Belt
    Advanced combinations, competition-level sparring, and mentoring newer students.
  • Brown Belt
    Pre-black belt. Demonstrates all previous material plus advanced kata and leadership qualities.
  • Junior Black Belt (Shodan)
    The highest junior rank. Demonstrates technical mastery, character, and commitment. A life achievement.

Gradings are held quarterly and assessed by our head instructors. Progress is based on technical skill, mat time, and character development, not just time served. Stripe assessments happen every 3-6 months between major gradings.

Built to Build Better Kids.

Our junior programs are far more than martial arts. We use structured training to build character traits that serve children for life. Our curriculum is designed in partnership with child development experts and experienced educators.

Focus and Attention

In an age of constant distraction, martial arts trains the mind to be fully present. Kids learn to focus on instruction, retain technique, and apply skills under gentle pressure. Teachers often report visible improvements in classroom attention.

Discipline and Respect

Every class begins and ends with a bow. Children learn to respect their instructors, peers, and themselves. The dojo has clear rules, consistent expectations, and positive reinforcement that builds self-regulation and follow-through.

Confidence Building

Belt progressions give kids tangible goals and visible achievement. Each class is a small win. Over months of training, shy, uncertain children become self-assured young people who walk taller, speak up, and back themselves.

Anti-Bullying Program

We teach children how to de-escalate conflict verbally, how to walk away with confidence, and - only if truly necessary - how to protect themselves. Our approach reduces bullying through empowerment, not aggression. Bullies target the unsure. We remove that target.
